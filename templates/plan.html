How does this work?

  1. Open a place from Places and press Add to my visit plan.
  2. Come back here. Use the arrows to change the order of your stops.
  3. Set your start time. Arrival and leaving times are updated automatically.
  4. Press Print plan to take your itinerary with you.

Times include travel at about 30 km/h, the suggested visit time at each place, and a {{ 15 }}-minute buffer after every stop. This planner covers a single day only.
